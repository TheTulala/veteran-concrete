Before you reach out

A few details help us give you a better estimate

You don't need plans or measurements to get started. A couple of sentences about what's there now and what you want is plenty.

Tell us whether this is new concrete, an addition to what you have, or a fix for a slab that's cracked, stained, flaking, or just tired. If you're thinking about a look, like a flagstone stamp, a wood plank texture, or a warmer color, mention it. We'll bring samples and ideas to the estimate.

If you live in a community with a homeowners association, check whether exterior concrete changes need design approval first. Many do, especially driveway widening and anything visible from the street. Your bid spells out size, pattern, and color, which is what most review committees ask to see.

Good to have handy

  • Where it isFront, back, side yard, pool area, or garage, and roughly how big.
  • What's there nowBare dirt, gravel, pavers, or existing concrete, and what shape it's in.
  • What you wantA plain slab, stamped, stained, textured, or not sure yet. Not sure is fine.
  • Your timingA date you're working toward, like a party, a pool build, or a home sale.

What to know before you call

Quick answers about estimates and how we work. Anything else, just ask.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the site, take measurements, and give you a complete bid at no cost and with no obligation. If the job isn't a fit for you, that's the end of it.

Do I need to be home for the estimate?

It helps. Walking the site together is the best way to talk through patterns, colors, and how you plan to use the space. If you can't be there, tell us when we call and we'll work out the details with you.

What should I put in the form?

A few sentences is plenty: what's there now, what you'd like, roughly how big, and your neighborhood or city. We'll follow up with any questions before we come out.

Do you work outside Las Vegas?

Yes. We work across the valley and Southern Nevada, including Henderson, Summerlin, Spring Valley and Enterprise, Pahrump, and Boulder City. If you're nearby but not listed, call and ask.

Are you licensed in Nevada?

Yes. Veteran Concrete holds Nevada contractor license #0094181 (C-08 Concrete). You can verify it, or any contractor's license, with the Nevada State Contractors Board at (702) 486‑1100.
