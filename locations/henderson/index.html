HOA country

Henderson concrete has to look good and get approved

In most Henderson neighborhoods, new concrete isn't just your call. It's your HOA's too.

Much of Henderson is master-planned, and most communities have design guidelines for anything visible from the street: driveway width, walkway layout, colors, and finishes. Starting work without approval can mean fines or tearing out new concrete. Nobody wants to pay for a driveway twice.

The fix is a bid that answers the committee's questions before they ask. Ours spells out the size and location of the work, the pattern, and the color, so you can attach it to your request and get a faster yes.

Out back, the job is usually about the pool. A plain gray deck gets painfully hot by noon in July. A light-colored knockdown or textured finish stays cooler and grips better when it's wet.

Before you submit to your HOA

  • Read the guidelinesLook for rules on driveway width, colors, and front-yard hardscape.
  • Get a detailed bidSize, location, pattern, and color, in writing.
  • Wait for the approvalWe schedule your pour after the approval letter comes through.

Does my HOA have to approve new concrete?

In most Henderson communities, yes, for anything visible from the street and often for backyard work too. Check your guidelines and get written approval before work starts. Our bid gives you the details the committee needs.

What's the best finish for a pool deck?

A light-colored textured finish, like knockdown or a stamped stone pattern, stays cooler and grips better when wet than smooth concrete. Sealing it with a UV-stable sealer every few years keeps the color looking fresh.

Can you add on to my existing patio?

Yes. New concrete next to old rarely matches perfectly, so many homeowners stamp or stain both together for one seamless look. We'll show you the options at the estimate.
