The real risk

Bad concrete usually starts with the wrong crew

Concrete is permanent. When a job goes sideways, you live with it every time you pull into the driveway or step onto the patio. Here's what goes wrong most often in Las Vegas, and what we do about it.

  1. 01

    The bid that disappears

    A low price, a half-finished slab, and a phone nobody answers. Nevada's contractor board regularly cites unlicensed concrete crews in Las Vegas, and if you hire one you lose access to the state's Residential Recovery Fund when things go wrong.

  2. 02

    Concrete rushed in the heat

    Desert sun pulls water out of fresh concrete fast. Rushed finishing and skipped curing show up months later as flaking, scaling, and cracks that wander across the slab.

  3. 03

    Tear-out as the only answer

    Some crews quote demolition for everything. Plenty of tired, stained, or dated slabs can be repaired and resurfaced in place for far less money and mess.

Before you tear it out

Concrete repair in Las Vegas that doesn't start with a jackhammer

Fixing substandard concrete without the hassle and cost of removing it is a big part of what we do. Hairline cracks, faded color, rough or spalled surfaces, and a plain gray slab can often be rebuilt right where they sit.

Resurfacing keeps your existing slab as the foundation and builds a new wearing surface on top. You skip the demolition, the dump fees, and the week of a torn-up yard. The result can be stamped, stained, or textured to look nothing like what was there before.

Not every slab qualifies. Concrete that is sinking, heaving, or broken into pieces needs to come out, and we'll tell you straight when that's the case.

Can you fix bad concrete without replacing it?

Often, yes. If the slab is structurally sound and not sinking or heaving, cracks can be repaired and the surface resurfaced with an overlay, then stained or stamped to look new. If the concrete is failing underneath, an overlay only hides the problem for a while, and we'll tell you that up front. Learn how concrete overlays work.

How much does stamped concrete cost in Las Vegas?

Price depends on square footage, the pattern, how many colors you want, site access, and how much base prep the ground needs. Simple single-color patterns cost less than multi-color stone or wood plank designs. We'll come out, look at the site, and give you a complete bid at no cost. See stamped concrete options.

Does decorative concrete hold up in Las Vegas heat?

Yes, when it's installed and sealed correctly. Desert sun is hard on color and sealer, so decorative concrete here needs a UV-stable sealer and a fresh coat every few years. Lighter colors also stay cooler underfoot around pools. We'll tell you exactly how to care for whatever we install.

Are you licensed in Nevada?

Yes. Veteran Concrete holds Nevada contractor license #0094181 (C-08 Concrete). You can verify it, or any contractor's license, with the Nevada State Contractors Board at (702) 486‑1100.

How long does a concrete patio take?

Most residential patios take a few working days, plus curing time before heavy use. One customer had a new patio and walkway poured between a Tuesday and a Thursday. Larger, stamped, or multi-color projects take longer, and you'll get a timeline with your bid.
