Get it right the first time

Why stamped concrete goes wrong, and how we avoid it

Stamped concrete is a timing game. Once the truck arrives, the crew has a short window to place, color, and stamp the slab before it sets, and in Las Vegas heat that window shrinks fast.

Short-handed or rushed crews leave the marks you've probably seen around town: seams that wander off line, stamp edges that never got detailed, blotchy color, and cracks that cut straight across the pattern because nobody planned where the joints should go. Once it's poured, those mistakes are permanent.

We plan the pattern, color, and joint layout before the pour, bring enough hands to stamp it while the concrete is ready, and finish the edges and steps by hand. Owner Justin Ford is on site for the pour, so the person who sold you the job is the one making sure it looks the way you were promised.

What a complete stamped concrete bid should cover

  • Base prep and compactionThe ground under the slab decides how long it lasts.
  • Forms and reinforcementClean lines, correct slope away from the house, and steel where it's needed.
  • Color planA base color plus a release color for depth and an antiqued look.
  • Joint layoutControl joints hidden in the pattern so cracks have a place to go.
  • SealerUV-stable, with slip-resistant grit for pool decks and walkways.

Built for the desert

Stamped concrete made for Las Vegas weather

  • Sun and color

    UV breaks down cheap sealers and washes out color. A UV-stable sealer and a reseal every few years keep the pattern rich.

How much does stamped concrete cost in Las Vegas?

Cost depends on square footage, the pattern, the number of colors, site access, and how much base prep the ground needs. A single-color pattern on an open, level yard costs less than a multi-color stone or wood plank design with tight edges and steps. We'll come out and give you a complete bid at no cost.

Is stamped concrete slippery?

Sealed stamped concrete can get slick when wet, especially around pools. Mixing a slip-resistant grit into the sealer and choosing a pattern with more texture keeps it safe underfoot. See our concrete texture options for extra grip.

How soon can I use a new stamped concrete patio?

Foot traffic is typically fine after a day or two. Wait about a week before putting heavy furniture or a grill on it, and longer before driving on a stamped driveway. You'll get exact timing for your project when the job is finished.

Does stamped concrete need to be resealed?

Yes. In the Las Vegas sun, plan on resealing every few years to keep the color rich and the surface protected. A fresh coat of sealer is quick and far cheaper than restoring faded concrete. Already faded? A stain or overlay can bring it back.
