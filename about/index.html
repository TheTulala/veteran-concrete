Who we are

What veteran owned means for your project

Most concrete problems trace back to someone cutting a corner they figured nobody would see. We were trained not to work that way.

Veteran Concrete is owned and operated by Justin Ford, a US Army veteran who has been in the concrete business since 2009. Over that time the work has covered just about everything concrete can do, from custom stamped patios and colored, textured finishes to foundations, walls, large commercial pours, and small patches.

That experience includes running a concrete pumping operation, with a simple promise: we put it where you want it. For you, that means a pump line can carry concrete over a block wall or through a side yard to a backyard patio, instead of a parade of wheelbarrows across your landscaping.

What hasn't changed is how the job gets run. The base prep and reinforcement nobody sees get the same attention as the finish everybody does. Bids are complete. Start dates are real. And if your slab can be saved with an overlay instead of a jackhammer, we'll tell you, even though replacing it would be the bigger ticket.

How we work

Discipline, integrity, accountability

Three words that sound good on a wall. Here's what they look like on your property.

Mission first, quality always.

How the crew approaches every job

  1. Discipline

    Concrete doesn't forgive shortcuts, especially in desert heat. Compacted base, proper forms and steel, and the right finishing and curing are done every time, on every size of job, whether or not anyone is watching.

  2. Integrity

    The bid you sign is the job you get. If we find something underground that changes the plan, you hear about it before we move forward, not on the invoice.

  3. Accountability

    Our name and our Nevada license are on the work. If something isn't right, you have a phone number that gets answered and a crew that comes back to make it right.
